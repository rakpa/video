## Description (4000)

```
VidCliply is the fastest way to save videos you own or have the rights to as a
ready-to-play MP4 — in the quality you choose, always with sound.

Paste a link, preview the video instantly, pick a resolution, and save. That's it.
No accounts, no watermarks, no clutter.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Save videos as a single MP4 with audio already merged — never a silent file.
• Choose your quality: 720p, 1080p Full HD, 2K, or 4K Ultra HD.
• Clip any section — set a start and end time to keep just the highlight you want.
• Instant preview — thumbnail, title, and details load the moment you paste a link.
• Clean output — no watermarks stamped on your file.

BUILT TO BE SIMPLE
• No sign-up and no subscription required to get started.
• A focused, distraction-free interface designed for one job: saving your video.
• Files are processed and delivered to your device — nothing is kept.

PLAY FAIR
VidCliply is a tool for saving content you own or are authorised to download —
your own uploads, Creative Commons material, and media you hold the rights to.
Please respect each platform's Terms of Service and applicable copyright law.

Questions or feedback? Reach us any time from the in-app Contact page.
```
